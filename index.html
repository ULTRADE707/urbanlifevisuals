About

I'm Erwin Urbano, a videographer and photographer based in the Bay Area. Through UrbanLifeVisuals I capture street life, candid moments, and stories for clients, from music videos to weddings.

Before the camera, I spent more than ten years in the U.S. Navy and field engineering. That work taught me to plan carefully, stay calm under pressure, and deliver on deadline. I bring the same discipline to every shoot and every edit.

I'm completing a B.A. in Communications & Media Technology at Academy of Art University (Fall 2026).
